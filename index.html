Check a link before you click it

Fake links in text messages, emails and QR codes are an easy way to steal passwords and bank details. SafeLink looks at the link for you and tells you if it is safe, risky or dangerous, with the reasons in simple words.

Why use SafeLink?

  • Free, with nothing to install.
  • Works with web addresses, full text messages and QR code images.
  • Explains each warning, so you learn what to look for.

    How it works

    1. Choose link or message, or QR code.
    2. Paste the text or upload the image.
    3. Press the check button.
    4. Read the rating and the reasons. Open the link only if it looks safe.
