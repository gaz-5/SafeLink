Check a link before you open it

Paste a link or a message, or upload a QR code picture. SafeLink tells you if it looks safe and shows why.

    No tool is 100% accurate. If you are unsure, don't enter any passwords.

    Don't paste personal information. A rating is a guide, not a guarantee.

    Want to know what we look for? Read how it works or the about page.

    Clear

    How it works

    1. Choose Link, Message or QR code.
    2. Paste the text or upload the picture.
    3. Press the check button and read the rating.
    4. Open the link only if the rating says it is safe.

    Spot a fake link

    These are the signs we look for. You can look for them yourself too.
