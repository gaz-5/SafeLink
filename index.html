Welcome to SafeLink

SafeLink helps you decide whether a link is safe before you open it. Scams often arrive as text messages, emails, or QR codes on posters.

  • Check a URL or message: paste a web address or a full SMS and get a safety rating.
  • Scan a QR code: upload a photo of the code and we check where it leads.
