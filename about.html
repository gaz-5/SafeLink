About SafeLink

SafeLink is a course project for CPIS-358 at King Abdulaziz University. It is a small website that helps people avoid scam links.

  • The problem
  • Our solution
  • Design choices
  • What we check
  • Project details

The problem

Scam messages are very common. A text that says "your parcel is waiting" or a QR code stuck on a poster can lead to a fake website that steals your password. It is hard to tell if a link is safe just by looking at it.

Our solution

SafeLink reads a link, a message or a QR code picture and gives one of three ratings: safe, be careful, or dangerous. Each rating comes with the reasons behind it.

Design choices

  • One task per tab: link, message or QR code.
  • Every box has a label that says what to type.
  • Ratings use color and words, so color alone is not the only signal.
  • Night mode and light mode, saved for the next visit.
  • The menu shows which page you are on.
  • Works with the keyboard and on a phone screen.

What we check

Warning signs and why they matter
Warning sign Why it matters
No HTTPS The connection is not protected.
Short links The real website is hidden.
Number addresses Real companies rarely use them.
Pressure words Scammers want you to act without thinking.
